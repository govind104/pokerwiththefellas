Item 8: big-index card faces

Today's 3D cards use the vendored SVG faces (left column). Their corner index (rank plus suit) is small, about 10% of the card height, and the middle is full of pips or face art. When a card lies flat on the felt a metre away, only a few pixels of that index survive. The proposal (right column) draws our own faces: a corner rank about 40% of the card width, a suit under it, the same index mirrored in the opposite corner, and one large suit in the middle. Court cards keep a simple framed letter instead of detailed art. It's the same idea as the "jumbo index" decks used on casino tables and in online poker.

Sizes below: 150 px (close-up), 64 px (about the size of a board card in the "double, same pitch" view at 1280×720) and 40 px (an opponent's cards), plus the 64 px card tilted as if lying on the table.
