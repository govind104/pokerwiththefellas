  • Top right: the board, five slots always shown. Unrevealed streets show face-down backs, so the flop is 3 up and 2 down. Pot and street go underneath. The Admin chip moves into the top-left cluster to make room.
  • Bottom left: one row per seat, with you at the bottom: balance (large), then name, live bet this street and a dealer-button, SB or BB badge. Rows in the list scheme get a gold edge when it's that seat's turn. Folded seats are dimmed. Revealed hole cards appear as mini cards at showdown, and only your own cards show before that.
  • Bottom right: actions as a prompt list with keyboard shortcuts (greyed out when not legal), replacing today's bottom-centre buttons. The raise amount input would sit next to Raise.
  • The 3D name plates over each seat would become redundant. Remove them, or keep only the name.
